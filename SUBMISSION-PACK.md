## Title

BasisQuorum — Declared-Basis Quorum with Transitive Correlation Accounting

## Notes / Description

BasisQuorum is a quorum primitive for declared evidence. An owner freezes a claim, independence standard, authorized reporters and threshold. Up to six append-only reports retain complete text, asserted basis and authenticated writer. GenLayer independently classifies every stance, every unordered basis pair and whole-set coverage. Deterministic connected components collapse shared or uncertain dependencies; exact copied basis text always merges. Uniform components count once, contradictions or unresolved context cannot certify, and a bridge report can reduce quorum. Every append immediately invalidates the live certificate. Validators rebuild and exactly compare the full canonical receipt. Readable decisions are final per revision; technical retries are bounded, history is immutable and retirement revokes live access. Includes 41 local tests, 102 receipt-leaf tamper cases and full-budget checks. Certificates concern declared bases, not verified origins or statistical independence.
